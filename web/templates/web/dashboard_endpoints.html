Why this table looks quiet

Less chrome, more signal for each endpoint.

  • Endpoints are grouped by environment and posture, not by hostname alone.
  • Posture is expressed as short labels such as Under pressure or Stable, not numeric scores.
  • The goal is to help you scan for “where to look next” in a few seconds.
  • In a live system, each row could link into alerts, recent activity, and attached rules.
