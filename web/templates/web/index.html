System map

From alert to endpoint to rule, without losing the thread.

Alert first

Start with an alert, not a dashboard.

The primary flow begins from an alert row, then fans out to the endpoint, rule, and recent activity in a single track. The design is quiet on purpose.

  • One column of text, no charts for the sake of charts.
  • Time, pressure, and impact expressed in words first.
  • Visuals added only where reading slows down.

Endpoints

A readable inventory.

Endpoints are grouped by environment and risk posture, not alphabetically. The surface suggests where to look without forcing it.

Rules

Intent over syntax.

Rules are named after behavior, not field names. The UI allows analysts to understand “why the rule exists” in a single sentence.
